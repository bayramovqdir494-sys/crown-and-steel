# Crown & Steel: Knight Wars

Original mobile-first 3D medieval battle game (Three.js). Lead the blue army against the red crown:
sword / mace / chain flail / axe / spear / bow, shield block + perfect parry, dodge roll, light & heavy combos,
mounted knights, boss duels, castle sieges, conquest flags, an armory shop, daily rewards, 17 star-rated levels across varied battlefields (meadow, forest, dusk, torch-lit night camp, desert canyon with a river, snowfield, and enclosed castle throne halls with columns, banners and torches): a great cavalry charge against spear walls and riding wedges, the Dread Knight in his throne hall, a multi-stage siege with a battering ram, catapult volleys and the flail-swinging Iron Warden, and the finale indoors in the Great Hall of Ravenhold. An Endless Arena survival mode keeps a saved high score.
Switch between your owned weapons mid-battle with the weapon tray (top-left) or the SWAP button; with the bow equipped a green SHOOT button appears: hold to draw, release to fire.
Enemies include soldiers, spearmen, shield-bearers, archers, knights, cavalry and bosses.
Your knight wears a heraldic cape that grows and gains gold trim and a crown crest as you upgrade Plate Armor, and the main menu plays a live skirmish in the background.

Controls: touch joystick + buttons (landscape), or WASD + mouse (left click = attack, hold = heavy attack, right mouse / F = block,
Shift = sprint, Space = dodge roll, Q = cycle weapon, 1-4 = pick weapon from the tray, hold left click / J with the bow = draw, release = fire, R = rally allies, E = horse charge (mounted levels), J = attack, Esc / P = pause).

All character, weapon and building meshes were generated with Hunyuan3D-2 (`/shape_generation`, shape only),
then colored, decimated and auto-rigged locally. Pine/birch/bush props are reused from our earlier game Spear Hunter.
CrazyGames SDK v3 is optional; the game runs standalone.
